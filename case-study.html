// 02 — The System

Claude runs your day.
You just show up.

Four automated workflows connect Notion, Zapier, and Claude — handling planning, prioritization, habit check-ins, and weekly reviews without any manual input.

01
⏰ Every day · 8:00 AM

Morning plan, generated automatically

Zapier reads your task list, top project, and energy level from Notion — sends everything to Claude — writes your focused daily plan back before you open your laptop.

02
➕ On new task added

Auto-prioritization — no scoring needed

Every new task triggers Claude to score it by impact, urgency, and energy required. The priority field in Notion updates automatically. You never manually rank a task again.

03
🌙 Every day · 9:00 PM

Honest habit check-in

Zapier reads your habit data and current streak — Claude gives a brief, honest check-in with one practical tip — written back to Notion with your streak counter updated.

04
📊 Every Sunday · 6:00 PM

Weekly review, ready Monday morning

Claude analyzes your full week — completed tasks, missed items, habits, points — and generates a review with pattern observations and next week's focus recommendation.

// 03 — AI Prompt System

4 prompts.
One for every moment.

Each prompt is engineered for a specific point in the productivity cycle — tuned for tone, length, and output format.

⏰ Daily · 8:00 AM

Morning Plan

Top 3 must-do tasks · 90-min deep work block · One sentence of motivational context · Under 150 words · Direct, not generic

➕ On new task

Task Prioritization

Impact score 1–10 · Urgency score · Energy level required · Priority ranking with one-line reasoning per task

🌙 Daily · 9:00 PM

Habit Check-in

Acknowledge what was done · One practical tip for missed habits · Streak status · Honest, under 50 words

📊 Sunday · 6:00 PM

Weekly Review

What went well · Patterns identified · Next week focus · Honest and specific — not just positive · Under 200 words
